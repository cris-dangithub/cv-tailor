# Learned preferences

Rules learned from the candidate's feedback on generated CVs. They are applied to every new
CV, but **the requirements of the offer always weigh more**: a preference never removes
something the offer asks for, and never adds something the pool does not prove.

Format:

```
## P-001 · YYYY-MM-DD · from application 000000
- Rule: what to do or avoid, stated so it can be applied to any offer.
- Why: the candidate's own words, short.
- Scope: all | language xx | kind of role
- Strength: firm (candidate said always/never) | soft (one-off remark)
```

When two rules conflict, the newer firm rule wins; mention the conflict to the candidate.
